Download GPX

Following or sharing a route also saves it to My routes, with its map area stored for weak signal.

Put it on your Forerunner 265

  1. Tap Share GPX and pick Garmin Connect if it's listed. Otherwise save it to Files, then on connect.garmin.com go to Training & Planning, Courses, Import.
  2. Save the course, then choose Send to Device.
  3. On the watch: Run, then hold Menu, Navigation, Courses, and pick it.

Naver Map and Apple Maps can't open GPX files. Use Follow on this phone, or share the file to Komoot, Strava or Gaia GPS.
