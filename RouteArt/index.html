Download GPX

Put it on your Forerunner 265

  1. Open Garmin Connect on a computer (connect.garmin.com), go to Training & Planning, then Courses, then Import, and choose the GPX file.
  2. Save the course, then choose Send to Device.
  3. On the watch: Run, then hold Menu, Navigation, Courses, and pick it.

Naver Map can't open GPX files. To follow it on your phone, open the file in an app such as Strava, Komoot or a GPX viewer.
