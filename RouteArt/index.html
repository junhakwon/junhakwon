Route Art

Pick a start point and a picture. Route Art fits the picture onto real streets and trails and gives you a GPX file for your Garmin.

Install Route Art: tap the Share button in Safari, then Add to Home Screen. It opens full screen like an app.

1Where are you starting?

You can also tap the map to set or move the start point. Not needed if you pick a place under Where.

Picture type

Bold, simple shapes make the best routes. If the shape isn't what you want, tap Paint the shape and colour in the part you want to run.

3Route settings

Or type any city, district or country. Bigger areas give more chances to find streets shaped like your picture, but the route may be far from you.

Is it okay if the route goes over mountains?

Seoul's hills and mountains have great trails, but they are slower, steeper and darker at night.

Set a start point, choose a picture and answer the mountain question.

Download GPX

Following or sharing a route also saves it to My routes.

Put it on your Forerunner 265

  1. Tap Share GPX and pick Garmin Connect if it's listed. Otherwise save it to Files, then on connect.garmin.com go to Training & Planning, Courses, Import.
  2. Save the course, then choose Send to Device.
  3. On the watch: Run, then hold Menu, Navigation, Courses, and pick it.

Naver Map and Apple Maps can't open GPX files. Use Follow on this phone, or share the file to Komoot, Strava or Gaia GPS.
